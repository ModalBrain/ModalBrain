Execution phases from today (Nov 14, 2026) through Dec 7, 2026, backed by 7 scheduled Google Tasks reminders.

Dual-Pillar Architecture

GitHub serves as the permanent archive for code, patches, and paper breakdowns, while Discord hosts real-time patch feedback, jam sessions, and weekly paper clubs.

Platform / Area Primary Role Recommended Structure & Scope
GitHub Organization Code, patches & curated index Start with three core repositories: awesome-modal-music (papers & resources), modal-patches (M4L & VCV files), and dedicated instrument repos.
GitHub Discussions Structured paper & design threads Categories for Papers (ISMIR, DAFx, NIME reviews), M4L-Devices, VCV-Patches, and Instrument-RFC.
Discord: Max for Live Ableton Live device creation Channels #m4l-devices and #max-gen-dsp for sharing .amxd tools alongside Max for Live and the Max for Live Library.
Discord: VCV & Modular Patching & C++ module DSP Channels #vcv-patches and #cardinal-dsp for VCV Rack, VCV Library, and DISTRHO Cardinal workflows.
Discord: Papers & Tuning Research & microtonal theory Channel #paper-reading (synced via GitHub webhook) and #tuning-scales for Scala (.scl) files, maqam, and radif tuning systems.

Day-One Seeding & Showcase

Launch with concrete, testable assets so the first wave of builders immediately sees the standard and depth of the community.

01 · STARTER KITS

Reference Patches & DSP

  • Curate open starter patches for Max for Live demonstrating modal resonators and scale mapping
  • Provide clean starter templates for DISTRHO Cardinal and VCV Rack
  • Include foundational open-source DSP building blocks (modal filter banks, zero-delay resonators)
02 · PAPERS

Research & Resources

  • Publish your own recent paper notes and implementations as the inaugural GitHub Discussion
  • Seed an annotated reading list from ISMIR, DAFx, and NIME
  • Host starter Scala tuning packs (.scl) for electronic modal composition
03 · TEMPLATES

Contribution Standards

  • Standard template for Max for Live submissions (.amxd + audio preview)
  • Standard template for VCV Rack patches (.vcv + required module list)
  • Paper review format: core problem, DSP method, and electronic music application

4-Phase Launch Timeline

A focused rollout from initial setup today to launching open-source community instrument builds.

Four-stage rollout from initial infrastructure to collaborative instrument construction (Nov 14 – Dec 7, 2026).

PHASE 1 · NOV 14 – 16, 2026

Infrastructure & Initial Seeding

Create the GitHub Organization, enable Discussions, configure Discord channels, and publish your first reviewed papers alongside foundational reference patches and starter templates.

PHASE 2 · NOV 19 – 23, 2026

Automation & First-Wave Outreach

Write CONTRIBUTING.md, wire GitHub webhooks to Discord's #paper-reading channel, and send targeted invites to active Max for Live developers and VCV Rack patchers.

PHASE 3 · NOV 30, 2026

First Paper & Patch Club

Host the inaugural live Discord session: break down one recent academic paper and run a live demo or patch walkthrough in VCV Rack and Max for Live.

PHASE 4 · DEC 07, 2026+

Instrument Construction Roadmap

Synthesize early member feedback and publish the first open instrument spec (e.g., a modal quantizer for M4L or a physical-modeling/modal module in Cardinal) for collaborative prototyping.

Growth Principle

Prioritize "paper + playable patch" pairings over raw member count in month one. Every research breakdown accompanied by a downloadable .amxd or .vcv file gives builders an immediate reason to join and contribute.
